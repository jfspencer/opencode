City council approves new transit plan after marathon session

The $2.4bn plan adds three tram lines and a bus rapid transit corridor by 2031.

By Jane Doe and John Roe January 15, 2025, 8:00 AM Updated 11:42 AM
ShareEmail

After more than nine hours of debate, the city council voted 9–4 late Tuesday to approve the Metro 2031 transit plan, the largest public-works commitment in the city's history.

The plan includes:

  • three new tram lines connecting the north, east and airport districts;
  • a 14 km bus rapid transit corridor along Main Street;
  • 200 additional bike-share docks.

“This is a generational investment,” said Mayor Ana Lima. “Our kids will thank us — or at least they'll get to school on time.”

Opponents argued the cost estimates were unrealistic. Councillor Ben Okafor noted that a 2019 light-rail project ran 38% over budget.

We are writing a blank cheque.

— Councillor Ben Okafor

What happens next

Construction tenders open in March. The first line, the Northern Loop, is scheduled to open in 2027.
